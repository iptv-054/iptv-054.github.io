Get started

Start watching in three simple steps

IPTV Player is a media player, not a content subscription service. Bring your own authorized sources to get started.

01

Add Your Source

Import a compatible M3U/M3U8 playlist URL or connect using the Xtream Codes details supplied by your authorized service provider.

02

Browse Your Content

Explore the channels, movies, series, and other media included in your source. Search for content and save your favorites for quick access.

03

Play Your Media

Select compatible content to start playback. Use supported player features, Picture-in-Picture, or casting when available.
