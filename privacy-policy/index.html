1. Information We Handle

The information involved in using the App depends on the features you choose and the sources you configure. Categories may include the following:

  • Playlist information: playlist names, playlist URLs, M3U/M3U8 source addresses, and related settings that you enter.
  • IPTV connection details: host URLs, usernames, passwords, and other credentials you provide to connect to compatible Xtream Codes services.
  • App preferences: settings, favorites, playlist organization, and an optional passcode used to protect access to supported playlist features.
  • Local media information: files or media that you choose to access through the App, subject to the permissions granted on your device.
  • Technical and diagnostic information: depending on the services integrated into the published version, information such as app version, device model, operating system version, crash reports, performance data, and diagnostic events may be processed.
  • Advertising or analytics information: if advertising or analytics services are integrated, their providers may process identifiers, app interaction events, and related technical information as described in their respective privacy policies and your available privacy settings.

We do not intend to collect information that is unrelated to providing, maintaining, securing, or improving the App's functionality.

2. Playlist and Account Details

The App allows you to configure your own IPTV sources. Depending on the connection method, you may enter:

  • A playlist name and M3U/M3U8 URL.
  • A service host or server URL.
  • A username and password for Xtream Codes access.
  • Other connection information required by your source.

These details are used to configure your playlist and request supported content or account information from the server you specify.

When you connect to a third-party IPTV server, that server may receive connection information, including your IP address and the requests necessary to provide the service. Its handling of information is governed by its own privacy practices.

We do not operate or control third-party IPTV providers and cannot determine how those providers independently collect or process information.

Important: Only add sources and credentials from providers you trust and are authorized to use. Do not share your account details with others.

3. Local Storage

The App may store playlist configurations, connection settings, favorites, preferences, and related information locally on your device to support convenient use between sessions.

If you enable an optional in-app passcode, it is intended to provide an additional layer of protection for supported playlist access within the App. This feature should not be considered a substitute for your device's screen lock or other security controls.

Local storage behavior depends on the app version and device environment. Unless a feature explicitly states otherwise, you should not assume that your playlists or preferences are backed up or synchronized across devices.

Uninstalling the App may remove locally stored information. Some operating-system backups or other device-level mechanisms may behave differently depending on your settings.

4. How Information Is Used

Information may be used for the following purposes:

  • Providing core functionality: importing playlists, connecting to compatible IPTV services, browsing available categories, and playing supported media.
  • Managing preferences: saving favorites, playlist settings, and other supported configurations.
  • Supporting playback: processing requests needed for streaming, local media playback, Picture-in- Picture, or supported casting functionality.
  • Maintaining and improving the App: diagnosing crashes, resolving technical issues, and improving reliability and performance where applicable.
  • Security and abuse prevention: protecting the App, investigating technical misuse, and maintaining service integrity.
  • Advertising and measurement: where applicable, supporting ads, measuring ad performance, and understanding app usage through integrated services.
  • Legal compliance: responding to valid legal obligations and protecting applicable rights.

We do not use your IPTV credentials to provide IPTV subscriptions or sell access to television channels.

5. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information as part of the App's core functionality. Information may be shared or processed in the following circumstances, where applicable:

  • IPTV providers you select: your device connects to the server or playlist URL you configure. That provider may process the connection information required to deliver its service.
  • Service providers: technical providers may process limited information to support hosting, diagnostics, analytics, advertising, or other services integrated into the App.
  • Legal requirements: information may be disclosed when required by law or in response to valid legal processes.
  • Protection of rights: information may be processed or disclosed when reasonably necessary to investigate fraud, address security incidents, or protect users and other parties.
  • Business changes: information may be transferred as part of a merger, acquisition, or transfer of relevant assets, subject to applicable law.

We do not independently publish your playlists or make your IPTV credentials publicly available.

6. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services to support certain features. Depending on the version distributed, these may include services for advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or app distribution.

When such services are integrated and active, their providers may process information under their own privacy policies. Their data practices, retention periods, and security measures are not controlled entirely by us.

The App may also connect to third-party IPTV servers and casting-compatible devices at your request. These connections are distinct from any analytics or advertising services.

Please review the privacy policies of relevant third-party services and IPTV providers before using them.

7. Permissions and Device Features

Certain Android permissions or system features may be required for specific functions. The permissions requested depend on the features available in your installed version.

Network Access

Network access is needed to retrieve online playlists, connect to compatible IPTV services, and stream content from sources you provide.

Local Media Access

The App may use Android's media or file-selection mechanisms to let you choose supported local files. Access is limited by the permissions and selection mechanisms provided by your device.

Picture-in-Picture

If supported and enabled, Picture-in-Picture allows compatible video playback to continue in a floating window. You can manage this feature through the relevant device settings.

Casting and External Displays

Casting or connecting to external devices may involve local network communication or the capabilities of compatible third-party services. Information exchanged with the receiving device depends on the casting method and the content you choose to play.

You can manage available permissions through Android settings. Restricting a permission may prevent the related feature from working.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures appropriate to the App's functionality to help protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration.

However, no method of electronic storage, transmission, or internet communication can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You are responsible for protecting your device, maintaining the confidentiality of your IPTV credentials, and choosing trustworthy playlist providers.

Where supported by the relevant services, encrypted connections may be used to communicate with remote servers. The security of a connection also depends on the server and URL you choose.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored locally may remain on your device until you remove it, clear the relevant app data, or uninstall the App, subject to operating-system behavior and backup settings.

Information processed by third-party IPTV providers, advertising partners, analytics providers, or other service providers is retained according to their respective policies and applicable requirements.

You can remove playlists or related settings through available in-app controls. You may also clear the App's data or uninstall it through Android settings. These actions may not delete information already received by third-party providers.

Where we control personal information held outside your device, you may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion, subject to applicable law and any legitimate retention obligations.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is a general-purpose media player and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us inappropriately, please contact us so we can review the matter and take appropriate action.

Parents and guardians should supervise the playlists, external services, and content sources accessed by children using the App.

11. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or other available remedies.

You can also take practical steps to manage your data:

  • Remove playlists you no longer use.
  • Update or remove saved connection details.
  • Revoke optional device permissions through Android settings.
  • Manage advertising preferences where supported by your device or services.
  • Clear app data or uninstall the App.
  • Contact us with a privacy-related request.

We will consider privacy requests in accordance with applicable laws. Some information may be retained where required or permitted by law, or where it is held independently by third-party providers.

12. International Data Transfers

If the App uses third-party service providers or connects to IPTV servers located in other countries, certain information may be processed outside your country of residence.

Where applicable, appropriate safeguards will be considered for international transfers as required by relevant privacy laws. The actual locations and safeguards depend on the services used.
